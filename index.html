Pianos are so cool

A piano is a keyboard instrument that produces sound when its keys are pressed, activating an action mechanism where hammers strike strings. Modern pianos have a row of 88 black and white keys—with the exception of specialized models like Bösendorfer and Stuart & Sons—tuned to a chromatic scale in equal temperament. A musician who specializes in playing the piano is called a pianist.
