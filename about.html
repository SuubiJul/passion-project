More About Pianos

Types of Pianos

  1. Grand Piano: The classic horizontal instrument known for rich sound projection.
  2. Upright Piano: A compact vertical design perfect for homes and teaching studios.
  3. Keyboard: Portable electronic instrument with synth options and volume control.
  4. Digital Piano: Emulates traditional piano feel and tone with modern features.
  5. Hybrid Piano: Combines acoustic wooden action mechanisms with digital audio.
